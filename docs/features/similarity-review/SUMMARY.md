# Internal artwork and identity similarity screening

**Status:** not-started

Batch-check fictional artwork and identities across explicit similarity dimensions. Preserve comparison evidence, route uncertain cases to deeper or human review, and support revision. Outcomes are internal screening decisions, never legal clearance.
